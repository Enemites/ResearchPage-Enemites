Research Methodology

How We Conduct
Our Research

Our research methodology combines rigorous scientific standards with real-world user data to validate cognitive enhancement claims.

Step 01

Literature Review & Hypothesis

Every research initiative starts with a comprehensive review of peer-reviewed literature. We map credible sources from journals like Nature, Science, NeurIPS, and Cell to form evidence-based hypotheses about cognitive enhancement mechanisms.

Step 02

Internal Data Collection

We instrument NovaX's platform to collect anonymized behavioral and performance data. Metrics include task completion rates, error patterns, session engagement, and cognitive load proxies — all collected with IRB-equivalent ethical standards.

Step 03

Statistical Analysis & Validation

Collected data undergoes rigorous statistical analysis including significance testing, effect size estimation, and cross-validation across user cohorts. We use pre-registered analysis plans to prevent p-hacking.

Step 04

Publication & Iteration

Findings are documented in citation-mapped research papers with full reference traceability. Results feed back into product development, creating a continuous improvement loop between science and engineering.
